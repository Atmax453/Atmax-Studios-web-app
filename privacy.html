Privacy Policy

ATMAX STUDIOS · Last updated 20 November 2026

ATMAX STUDIOS ("we", "us") runs the streaming website at atmaxstudios.com from Ghana. This policy explains what information we collect when you use it, why, and the choices you have. We handle personal data in line with Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843).

1. Information we collect

  • Account details: your email address, and your password (stored securely by our sign-in provider; we cannot read it). If you choose "Continue with Google", we receive your email address and basic profile from Google. We do not receive your Google password.
  • Viewing activity: which films you watch, where you stopped, your list, your ratings and comments, and view counts. Profile names you create inside your account.
  • Payment records: when you pay for a film, our payment provider Paystack handles your Mobile Money or card details. We never see or store your card number or Mobile Money PIN. We keep the email used, the film, the amount, the date, the payment method type, and a payment reference so we can give you access and answer queries.
  • Device information: a random device code stored in your browser, used to keep one account to one device, and the time you last used the site.
  • Technical data: basic information your browser sends (such as browser type and approximate connection details), used to keep the site working and secure.

2. How we use it

  • To create your account, sign you in and keep your account secure.
  • To let you watch films, continue where you stopped, and keep your list.
  • To process payments, give you access for the period you paid for, and handle refunds or renewal reminders.
  • To count views, understand what is popular, and pay film owners their share.
  • To prevent sharing of accounts, copying of films and misuse of the site.

3. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal data. We use these service providers to run the site, and they process data for us:

  • Supabase – hosts our database, sign-in and file storage.
  • Paystack – processes payments and payouts.
  • Google – only if you choose to sign in with Google; and, where a film is played from YouTube, YouTube's player.
  • GitHub Pages and our domain provider – deliver the website to your device.

Film owners see only totals (for example, how many views and how much revenue a film earned), and, where we show it, the email of paying viewers is visible only to ATMAX STUDIOS administrators. We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

4. Where your data is stored

Our providers may store and process data on servers outside Ghana. We choose providers that protect data with reasonable security measures such as encryption in transit and access controls.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your account and viewing information while your account is active. Payment records are kept as long as needed for accounting and legal reasons. You can ask us to delete your account at any time (see section 7).

6. Cookies and local storage

We use your browser's local storage to keep you signed in, remember your device code and remember small preferences such as the one-time intro. We do not use advertising cookies.

7. Your choices and rights

You can ask us to show you the personal data we hold about you, correct it, or delete your account and data, and you can object to how we use it. Email us at the address below and we will respond within a reasonable time. Some payment records may need to be kept for legal reasons.

8. Children

The site is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. Parents or guardians who believe a child has given us data can contact us to have it removed.

9. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your data, including restricted access to our database and private storage for paid films. No system is completely secure, so please use a strong, unique password and keep it private.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date above. Continued use of the site after a change means you accept the updated policy.
