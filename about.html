ATMAX STUDIOS

Film streaming from Ghana · atmaxstudios.com

ATMAX STUDIOS is a media production agency and film streaming website based in Ghana. We publish films and series made by ATMAX STUDIOS and partner filmmakers so that audiences can watch them online, on a phone, laptop or TV.

What you can do on the website

  • Browse the film catalogue, trailers and new releases without signing in.
  • Create an account to watch films, keep a personal list, rate films and continue where you stopped.
  • Watch free films after you sign in, or pay for a film you want to watch for a set period (such as 1 week, 1 month or 1 year) using Mobile Money or card.
  • Sign in on a TV by scanning a code with your phone.

Why we ask you to sign in with Google

You can create an account with an email address and password, or choose Continue with Google. When you use Google, the website receives only your email address and basic profile from your Google Account. We use it for one purpose: to create your ATMAX STUDIOS account and sign you in, so your list, viewing progress and purchases are kept safely under your account. We do not read your Google contacts, files or emails, we do not post for you, and we do not sell your information.
